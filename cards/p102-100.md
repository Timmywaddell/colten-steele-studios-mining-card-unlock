# Project Queen: Windows gaming and Godot on a budget P102-100

**Colten Steele Studios research update — 27 November 2026**

A ฿1,430 mining-oriented NVIDIA P102-100 10 GB is now rendering GTA V under Windows and our own Godot-based racing/city development project. A separate GT 1030 drives the display. This is a functional demonstration, not a claim of a world-first or a universal driver unlock.

Test system: Intel Core i3-9100F, 8 GB system RAM, P102-100 10 GB, GT 1030 display, NVIDIA driver version 582.78. The driver identifies the mining GPU as GTX 1080 Ti in Windows device enumeration; NVIDIA monitoring identifies the physical P102.

## Windows graphics research

The native D3D11 probe passed on the GT 1030 but failed on the P102. Our game-local DXVK 2.6.2 experiment achieved working Vulkan rendering on the P102 while the GT 1030 drove the monitor. No Windows system DLL replacement was used. A two-frame DXVK queue limit was the latest performance experiment; VSync remained disabled and graphics settings were unchanged during that comparison. Owner observations improved from roughly 60–65 to 70–75 FPS; the route was not controlled, so this is not a quantified causal speedup. Both installed display-driver packages and the working launch configuration have been archived privately for recovery.

## Our own game development

Colten Steele Studios is developing a Godot-based driving environment, original vehicle assets and AI-driving/telemetry workflows. Queen demonstrated the Vulkan rendering path, moving traffic and working traffic signals. The owner approved improved night silhouettes and moving reflections; four relevant lighting/traffic scripts were transferred to the Studio project and passed Godot script checks. A full Studio visual and gameplay regression remains pending. Our work includes developing independent game assets and comparing driving behavior with reference gameplay, not redistributing commercial game assets.
