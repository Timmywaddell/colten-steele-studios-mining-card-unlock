# Research note 01 — NVIDIA P104-100 real PCIe Gen2 x4 on Linux

**Status: reboot-reproducible physical PCIe Gen2 x4 unlock, independently verified.** A fresh Linux boot returned the GPU to factory Gen1 x4; our validated one-shot manual service restored real Gen2 x4 and the approximately doubled pinned-memory transfer speed. Automatic unattended activation at boot is not claimed. See the [boot and reboot comparison](../docs/reboot-status.md) for the exact accepted test stages.

## Hardware and environment

- NVIDIA P104-100 8 GB, GP104, PCI ID 10DE:1B87
- ASUS B250 Mining Expert; Intel Core i5-7500; 8 GB system RAM
- Ubuntu 24.04.5.1 on isolated persistent external test media, kernel 7.0.0-31-generic
- Proprietary NVIDIA driver 580.196.07 adapted for graphics compatibility
- Intel HD 630 as physical display; NVIDIA P104 used for CUDA/PRIME offload
- GPU fan held at 60%, front intake at full speed during activation

## Physical link verification

Raw link-status register:
- Before: `LNKSTA=0x1041` (Gen1 x4).
- After controlled one-shot retrain: `LNKSTA=0x1042` (Gen2 x4).
- `LNKCAP2=0x00000006` after activation.
- The temporary probe hook was removed after retraining. CUDA initialization did not restore Gen1.

These are reads of the negotiated hardware state, not merely driver-advertised capabilities. Physical x16, Gen3, extra VRAM, more shaders and VBIOS flashing were **not** demonstrated.

## Pinned CUDA benchmark

Exactly five 8 MiB pinned-memory transfer repetitions each direction per run. Median GB/s:

| Condition | H2D | D2H |
|---|---:|---:|
| Gen1 x4 | 0.784 | 0.830 |
| First Gen2 x4 | 1.544 | 1.665 |
| Repeat Gen2 x4 | 1.536 | 1.672 |
| After reboot + manual Gen2 reactivation | 1.544 | 1.669 |

Round-trip correctness check passed. See [raw published numerical summary](../data/p104-gen2-bandwidth.csv).

## Cooling and recovery discovery

Stopping GNOME could revert an automatic fan controller. Keeping NVIDIA Legacy Persistence Mode active together with explicit NVML fan control maintained the 60% GPU fan during a bounded driver rebind. The default on-disk NVIDIA driver remained unchanged; the experimental kernel-module candidate was loaded in-memory. A manual one-shot reactivation service succeeded after a real fresh reboot.

**The unlock is not unattended boot-persistent.** A normal cold boot restores Gen1 x4; human-supervised activation is required. The more aggressive upstream auto-enable procedure was not promoted on this specific machine.

## Important limits

This measures the CPU↔GPU bus, not AI token throughput, image-generation rate or gaming FPS. The roughly 2x speed claim is valid **only for this pinned CUDA transfer benchmark**. Long-running general-purpose GPU stability and a guarantee across every P104 revision were not established by these short tests.
